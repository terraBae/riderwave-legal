Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 · Applies to Rider Wave for iPhone

Plain-language summary: Rider Wave does not have a server or account system. Everything the app records about you — your rides, routes, hazard reports, and posts — stays on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to us, because there is nowhere for it to go.

1. What this app is

Rider Wave is a local-first motorcycle navigation and ride-tracking app under active development. It has no backend server, no user accounts, and no sign-in. All app data is created and stored on your iPhone.

2. Data stored on your iPhone

The app stores the following in its own private storage on your iPhone. None of it is sent to any Rider Wave server, because none exists:

  • Profile information you enter (name, rider icon, bike/garage entries)
  • Saved routes and ride history (GPS tracks, distance, time, speed)
  • Hazard reports and posts ("waves") you create
  • Saved places (home, work, recent searches)
  • App settings

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, iOS includes this data in those backups, as it does for most apps. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Your data in the app, or by deleting the app.

3. Location data

With your permission, Rider Wave uses your iPhone's location to show your position on the map, give turn-by-turn directions, and record your ride while the app is open. It asks for location only while the app is in use — it does not track you in the background or while the screen is locked. Your location is not sent to or stored on any Rider Wave server.

4. Third-party services this app talks to

To provide maps, search, routing, and weather, Rider Wave sends requests directly from your iPhone to independent services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — search and addresses
  • Valhalla and OSRM — routing
  • Overpass API — places nearby
  • Open-Meteo — weather
  • U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) — weather alerts
  • RainViewer — radar
  • Map tile providers — OpenStreetMap-based and Esri sources

Requests for weather, nearby search, and routes include the map coordinates needed to answer them. Rider Wave uses no API key or account with any of these services.

5. Music

If you allow access to Apple Music, Rider Wave shows the song playing in the Music app (title, artist, artwork, and position) on the map and sends play, pause, skip, and seek commands to it. It can also check which supported music apps are installed, so you can choose one to open from the map. For other music apps, iPhone only lets Rider Wave know that some audio is playing — never what it is. No music-service login is used, and none of this leaves your iPhone.

6. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising

Rider Wave has no user accounts, sign-in, or cloud-based social features, and includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking software.

7. Children's privacy

Rider Wave is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. With no accounts and no server, there is no way for personal information to reach us from any user.

8. Changes to this policy

Rider Wave is under active development. Planned features — such as a shared backend or user accounts — do not exist yet and are not described here as current fact. If a change affects how data is collected, stored, or transmitted, this policy will be updated first.
