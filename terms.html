2. What Rider Wave is

Rider Wave is a currently free, beta/testing-stage motorcycle navigation and ride-tracking app for Android. It provides mapping, turn-by-turn navigation, ride recording, weather information, and local (on-device) social/hazard features. As a beta product, features may change, be added, be removed, or behave unexpectedly without notice.

5a. Accounts and sharing (beta)

Rider Wave accounts, profiles, business profiles and community features are optional and in beta testing. When you create an account, the app asks you to accept these Terms and the Privacy Policy first; creating it confirms that you have. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account; by creating one you confirm that you are 16 or older. The privacy settings in the app are for testing only and may not work. Until public release, treat anything you add to an account as visible to other riders, and do not create an account if that is not acceptable to you. Section 13 of the Privacy Policy explains this in full.

5b. Routes you publish

If you publish a route to the route library, you confirm that it is yours to share, that it does not cross private land or a closed road, and that it does not ask riders to break the law. Routes, ratings and comments are reviewed and may be refused or removed, and your ratings and comments are shown with your handle. The app leaves the first and last 500 metres (about a third of a mile) off a published route so a home or garage at either end is not shown. Anyone who rides a library route does so at their own risk: roads change, close and differ from the map, and a route in the library is not a promise that it is safe, open or legal.
